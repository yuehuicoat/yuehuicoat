1. Scope & controller

This Privacy Policy applies to the website yuehuicoat.com and to all mobile applications published by Yuehui Coating Equipment (Hong Kong) Co., Limited (“Yuehui”, “we”, “us”, “our”) on Google Play, the Apple App Store and any other app store we may distribute through in the future. It covers personal data collected through these surfaces, as well as data collected during the course of providing our coating-equipment, technical-consulting, spare-parts and field-service offerings.

The data controller for the processing described in this policy is Yuehui Coating Equipment (Hong Kong) Co., Limited, a private company limited by shares, incorporated in Hong Kong, with its registered office at 17/F Kam Sang Building, 255-257 Des Voeux Road C, Sheung Wan, Hong Kong.

Where local law requires us to appoint a local representative (for example under the EU GDPR or the UK GDPR), the relevant details are set out in section 13.

2. Definitions

  • “Personal data” means any information that identifies, or can reasonably be used to identify, an individual.
  • “Processing” means any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure or erasure.
  • “Service” means our website, our mobile applications and any related customer-service interaction.
  • “SDK” means a third-party software development kit integrated into our mobile applications.
  • “End user” or “you” means any individual who interacts with our Service.

3. Data we collect

We collect the following categories of personal data, depending on which Service you use and how you interact with it.

3.1 Data you give us directly

  • Identity & contact data: name, business email, phone number, job title, employer, country.
  • Account credentials: email and hashed password for the Yuehui mobile app, plus your chosen language and time zone.
  • Communications data: the content of emails, contact-form submissions, support tickets, recorded calls (where permitted by law) and chat messages with our team.
  • Quotation data: details you share with us about a coating project, including substrate, throughput, finish targets, factory location, and any documents you upload.
  • Payment data: billing address, tax identifier and the last four digits of any payment card. We do not store full card numbers on our systems — these are tokenised by our PCI-DSS compliant payment processor.

3.2 Data we collect automatically

  • Device & log data: IP address, device type, operating system, browser type and version, locale, screen size, the URLs you came from and went to, and timestamps.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, features used, approximate click coordinates, scroll depth, app screens opened, jobs logged, defect photos taken, time spent on each screen.
  • Approximate location: country and city-level location derived from your IP address, or precise location if you grant the mobile app explicit permission.
  • Cookies & similar: as described in section 8.

3.3 Data from third parties

  • Business contact information from trade-show registrations, distributor referrals and public corporate registries.
  • Aggregated demographic and interest data from advertising networks — but only where you have given the relevant consent in the relevant jurisdiction.

3.4 Data we do not collect

We do not knowingly collect special categories of data (such as racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, trade-union membership, genetic or biometric data, health data, or data concerning sexual orientation) through the Service. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 16 — see section 14.

4. How we use data

We use personal data for the following purposes:

  1. To operate the Service. Authenticating your account, syncing data across devices, providing customer support, and remembering your preferences.
  2. To fulfil our contract with you. Preparing quotations, processing orders, shipping equipment and parts, scheduling installation and field-service visits, and issuing invoices.
  3. To improve the Service. Understanding which features are used most, diagnosing performance issues, and prioritising engineering investment.
  4. To communicate with you. Sending transactional emails (order confirmations, service updates, security alerts), responding to your enquiries, and — where you have opted in — sending the monthly Yuehui dispatch newsletter and product updates.
  5. To show advertising in our mobile apps. Displaying and measuring ads served by the networks described in section 5. The app does not show advertising to users on a paid plan.
  6. To comply with our legal obligations. Keeping accounting records, responding to lawful requests from public authorities, and meeting export-control and sanctions-screening requirements.
  7. To protect our legitimate interests. Detecting and preventing fraud or abuse of the Service, enforcing our Terms of Service, and defending legal claims — where these interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms.

5. Legal bases for processing (EU / EEA / UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction that requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on the following bases under the GDPR and UK GDPR:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — for processing needed to provide the Service, fulfil a quotation, deliver equipment, or carry out a service engagement.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — for product analytics, security, fraud prevention and basic direct marketing to existing customers, balanced against your rights and freedoms.
  • Compliance with a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) — for accounting, tax, sanctions screening and responding to lawful authority requests.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — for non-essential cookies, for personalised advertising, and for the Yuehui dispatch newsletter. You can withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

7. Ad formats we serve

The Yuehui mobile apps integrate the following ad formats, where supported by the device and the ad network:

  • Open-screen (splash) ads. Full-screen static or video ads shown during natural app launch transitions, typically 3 to 6 seconds. Skippable after 5 seconds on most networks.
  • Rewarded video ads. Opt-in full-screen video ads that offer the user a tangible in-app reward (for example, an additional “Defect Analysis” credit) in exchange for watching the ad to completion.
  • Interstitial ads. Full-screen ads shown at natural transition points (for example, between job-card views). Dismissible with a close button after 5 seconds.
  • Banner ads. Rectangular ads shown at the top or bottom of selected app screens. Always dismissible; never blocking primary functionality.
  • Native ads. Ads rendered in the visual style of the surrounding content, clearly labelled “Sponsored” or “Ad” in line with the IAB Native Advertising Playbook.

We do not serve the following ad types anywhere in the Service: (a) deceptive native ads that mimic operating-system or other app notifications; (b) ads that auto-play with sound without an opt-in; (c) ads for products illegal in the user’s jurisdiction; (d) ads that target children under 16 (see section 14); (e) ads that collect personal data through embedded forms.

8. Cookies & similar technologies

This website uses cookies and similar local-storage technologies. We classify them as follows:

  • Strictly necessary cookies. Required for the website to function — for example, the cookie that remembers your choice on the cookie banner. These are always on and require no consent in most jurisdictions.
  • Functional cookies. Remember preferences such as your language and time zone. On by default; you can disable them in the cookie banner.
  • Analytics cookies. First-party aggregated analytics that help us understand how visitors use the website. Disabled until you opt in.
  • Marketing cookies. Used by the advertising networks described in section 6 to measure and personalise advertising on this website. Disabled until you opt in.

You can change your choices at any time by clicking the “Privacy choices” link in the website footer.

9. Sharing & third parties

We share personal data with the following categories of recipients:

  • Service providers that help us operate the Service — for example, cloud hosting (AWS, Google Cloud), email delivery (Postmark, SendGrid), payment processing (Stripe), customer support (Zendesk) and analytics (first-party only).
  • Advertising partners as described in section 6, but only where you have given the relevant consent.
  • Logistics partners — couriers, freight forwarders and customs brokers — for the purpose of delivering equipment, parts and consumables you have ordered.
  • Professional advisers — auditors, lawyers, tax advisers — under confidentiality.
  • Public authorities where we are required to do so by law, or to respond to a valid court order.
  • Acquirers and investors in the event of a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of all or part of our business, subject to confidentiality and a duty to notify you.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share personal data with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes beyond what is described in section 6.

10. International transfers

We are a Hong Kong company. Personal data may be transferred to and processed in Hong Kong, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, the United States, Singapore and other jurisdictions in which our service providers or advertising partners operate.

Where we transfer personal data out of the EEA, the UK, mainland China, Brazil or other jurisdictions with restrictions on international transfers, we rely on appropriate safeguards — for example, the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA), or equivalent contractual mechanisms in other jurisdictions. You can request a copy of the relevant safeguards by emailing support@yuehuicoat.com.

The Hong Kong Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (PCPD) has issued guidance on cross-border transfers, which we follow. Our internal transfer mechanism includes: (a) data-minimisation at the source; (b) pseudonymisation where feasible; (c) encryption in transit and at rest; (d) contractually defined retention windows; and (e) documented data-subject-request workflows.

11. Retention

We keep personal data for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, and then delete or anonymise it. Specific retention periods include:

  • Account data: for the life of the account, plus 24 months after closure (for re-activation and customer-support continuity).
  • Quotation & order data: 7 years from the date of the transaction, to comply with accounting and tax obligations.
  • Service records & field reports: 7 years from the date of the visit, to support warranty claims and product-safety notifications.
  • Support tickets & chat transcripts: 3 years from the last interaction, to support ongoing customer relationships.
  • Analytics data: aggregated and anonymised after 26 months.
  • Advertising identifiers: reset on request; raw event-level data retained by ad partners for up to 13 months in line with their own policies.

12. Your rights

Subject to your jurisdiction, you have some or all of the following rights:

  • Right of access — to obtain confirmation of whether we process your data and a copy of that data.
  • Right of rectification — to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right of erasure (“right to be forgotten”) — to have your data deleted in defined circumstances.
  • Right to restrict processing — to limit how we use your data in defined circumstances.
  • Right to data portability — to receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — to object to processing based on legitimate interests, including direct marketing.
  • Right to withdraw consent — without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority in your jurisdiction.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@yuehuicoat.com with the subject line “Privacy request”. We will respond within 30 days, or sooner where required by local law. We may need to verify your identity before acting on your request.

14. Children’s privacy & age restrictions

The Service is intended for professional users aged 16 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from, or serve advertising to, anyone under 16.

  • EU/EEA/UK: we follow the age of digital consent set by each member state (typically 13–16).
  • United States (COPPA): the Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.
  • Mainland China (PIPL): the age of digital consent is 14.
  • South Korea (PIPA): the age of digital consent is 14.
  • Australia: the age of digital consent is 15 (under the Online Safety Act 2021).
  • Brazil (LGPD): the age of digital consent is 12, with parental consent required up to 18.

All advertising networks we work with are configured to disable behavioural advertising for users we know to be under 16, in line with Google Play’s “Families” policy and the Apple App Store’s “Kids Category” rules. If you believe we have collected data from a child in error, please email support@yuehuicoat.com and we will delete it promptly.

16. Security

We take the security of your personal data seriously. Our technical and organisational measures include encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256), role-based access control, full audit logging, multi-factor authentication for all staff with administrative access, regular penetration testing by an independent CREST-accredited firm, and ISO/IEC 27001-aligned information-security policies.

Despite our efforts, no system is 100% secure. If we become aware of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the relevant supervisory authority in accordance with applicable law.
