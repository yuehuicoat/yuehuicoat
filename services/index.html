Our services

A complete coating ecosystem — equipment, expertise and software.

Six tightly integrated practice areas cover everything from procurement and engineering to ongoing operations. Each one is led by senior specialists with decades of production-line experience across Asia, Europe and the Americas.

Discuss your project See all six
Service 01

Coating equipment supply

We curate, configure and supply the equipment that makes a finishing line. Every machine is selected for its quality, throughput, energy profile and the maturity of the manufacturer’s service network — then tailored to your substrate, finish spec and target output.

Powder coating lines

Complete booths, recovery systems, fluidised-bed powder containers, electrostatic guns, fast-colour-change modules and integrated curing ovens — built for low reject rates and high first-pass yield.

Liquid spray systems

Airless, air-assisted airless, HVLP and electrostatic liquid systems for solvent and waterborne paints — with heated hose technology, plural-component proportioners and closed-loop viscosity control.

Pretreatment & drying

Multi-stage iron phosphate, zirconium and nano-ceramic pretreatment, with closed-loop rinsing, deionised-water make-up and energy-recovery drying tunnels.

Conveyors & automation

Power-and-free, overhead monorail, belt and skid conveyors, indexing tables and robotic applicators — engineered in CAD and balanced to your cycle time.

Service 02

Import, export & wholesale

A full-service cross-border trade desk for OEMs, distributors and end users. We handle documentation, customs, freight and last-mile delivery to more than 60 countries.

OEM distribution

Authorised distributor relationships with leading Asian and European manufacturers of coating equipment, industrial machinery and accessories — with full warranty and aftermarket coverage.

Customs & compliance

HS code classification, certificates of origin, EUR.1, ATIGA, RCEP, US Customs bonds, REACH and TSCA declarations, plus dual-use export licensing where required.

Freight & Incoterms

EXW, FOB, CIF, DAP and DDP movements across sea, air and road — with marine insurance, container loading supervision and milestone tracking in our app.

Bulk supply & wholesale

Recurring supply contracts for distributors, with vendor-managed inventory, forecast sharing and price-locked annual frameworks.

Service 03

Installation, repair & maintenance

Multilingual field engineers deliver mechanical installation, electrical wiring, pneumatic plumbing, PLC commissioning, line balancing, scheduled preventive maintenance and 24/7 emergency repair.

On-site installation

Mechanical rigging, alignment, levelling, electrical and pneumatic install, PLC programming and full line commissioning with documented SAT sign-off.

Preventive maintenance

Scheduled visits, condition-based replacement of wear parts, calibration of gauges and sensors, and full digital service reports archived in our mobile app.

24/7 emergency repair

Hotline answered by a senior engineer in under three rings, with regional spares depots in Hong Kong, Rotterdam, Houston and São Paulo for rapid response.

Relocation & refurbishment

Dismantle, refurbish, transport and recommission of used coating lines — including laser alignment, conveyor re-tensioning and full PLC migration.

Service 04

Technical consulting

Engagements that pay back in the first quarter. From a one-week finish defect investigation to a multi-year process-improvement program, our senior engineers work side-by-side with your team.

Process audits

Five-day on-site audit covering pretreatment, application, curing, conveyor and quality control — with a prioritised 90-day improvement roadmap.

Defect analysis

Root-cause investigations of orange peel, cratering, pinholing, outgassing, adhesion failure and colour drift — using FTIR, cross-section microscopy and cure profiling.

Yield & OEE programs

Multi-month programs that combine line balancing, changeover reduction, statistical process control and operator training to lift first-pass yield.

Regulatory advisory

Coatings VOC compliance (EPA, EU Directive 2004/42/CE, GB 30981), REACH and TSCA substance management, and ISO 9001 / IATF 16949 audit prep.

Service 05

Parts & accessories

OEM-equivalent spares and consumables stocked in Hong Kong, with regional forward stock in Rotterdam, Houston and São Paulo. Most orders ship the next business day.

Spray gun parts

Nozzles, electrodes, air caps, fluid tips, gun bodies and trigger kits for the leading powder and liquid gun families — all traceable by serial number.

Powder recovery

Filter membranes, sieve stacks, cyclone bodies, after-filters, vacuum blowers and reclaim augers — engineered to maintain first-pass transfer efficiency.

Pumps & fluid handling

2K proportioning pumps, diaphragm pumps, heated hose assemblies, fluid regulators and viscosity controllers for waterborne and solventborne lines.

Conveyor components

Chain, trolleys, drive units, take-ups, bearings, lubricators, festoon systems and load bars — sized to your conveyor OEM and duty cycle.

Service 06 · Mobile management

The Yuehui mobile app, in your pocket and on the line.

A complete operations console for plant managers, shift supervisors and field engineers. Available on Google Play and the App Store, with localized onboarding for 14 languages and offline-first design.

  • ✦ Live line telemetry. Real-time OEE, throughput, cure-oven temperature, atomization pressure and powder consumption with five-second refresh.
  • ✦ Job & defect tracking. Photograph a finish defect, log it in two taps, and dispatch the right senior engineer directly from the same screen.
  • ✦ Spare-parts ordering. Search the global warehouse, reserve a batch, and watch it move from pick to delivered with full chain-of-custody.
  • ✦ Multi-plant roll-up. Compare OEE across plants, drill into the slowest shift, and export weekly briefings as a shareable PDF.
  • ✦ Offline-first. Capture defects, log jobs and place orders even when the plant Wi-Fi is down. Data syncs the moment a connection is back.
  • ✦ Privacy & compliance. Built-in support for GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, PIPL and Singapore PDPA — with full ad-network disclosures in our app-ads.txt and Privacy Policy.
