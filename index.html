Mobile Management

Run every coating line from your pocket.

The Yuehui app is a full operations console for production managers, shift supervisors and field engineers. Pair it with our smart sensors and the entire finishing floor becomes a real-time, mobile-first dashboard.

  • ✦ Live line telemetry. Throughput, cure oven temperature, atomization pressure and powder consumption — refreshed every five seconds.
  • ✦ Job & defect tracking. Photograph a finish defect on the line, log it in two taps, and dispatch a senior engineer directly from the same screen.
  • ✦ Spare-parts ordering. Search the Hong Kong warehouse, reserve a batch, and watch it move from pick to delivered with full chain-of-custody.
  • ✦ Multi-plant roll-up. Compare OEE across plants, drill into the slowest shift, and export weekly briefings in one shareable PDF.
