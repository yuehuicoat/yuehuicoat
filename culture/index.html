Our culture

Engineering with craft, curiosity and a clear point of view.

Yuehui is a multicultural team of senior engineers, field technicians, software designers and trade specialists. We are bound together by a single belief — that the right coating line, designed with care, is a beautiful thing.

Our mission

To make world-class coating technology accessible to every plant, everywhere.

For two decades the surface-finishing industry has been split between a few global incumbents and a long tail of regional suppliers. The incumbents ship the best technology, but only to the biggest plants. The regionals are responsive, but cannot offer the same quality. We exist to close that gap — to bring the same engineering depth, the same software and the same spare-parts coverage to every customer, regardless of size.

What we live by

Six values, in plain English.

Trust first

Every quote, every shipment, every service report — written down, kept on file, and reproducible. We earn the right to be your long-term partner.

Quality without compromise

We choose manufacturers whose service networks are as strong as their products. We refuse to ship anything that does not pass our own incoming inspection.

Innovation, in the field

We do not innovate for the sake of it. Every feature in our app, every new piece of equipment, must earn its place on a real production line.

One planet, one standard

The same quality bar in Hong Kong, Rotterdam, Houston and São Paulo. The same environmental and safety standards, in every market we serve.

Plain language

We translate complex coating science into decisions a plant manager can act on. No jargon, no hand-waving, no surprises in the invoice.

Service, not sales

Our account managers are senior engineers first. They would rather lose a deal than sell you the wrong machine — and they will tell you which one is right.

Our story

A short history, told in milestones.

2009

Founded in Sheung Wan

Three engineers, one small office above a tea shop, and a single conviction: that the surface-finishing industry deserved better technology partners.

2013

First European project

Designed and commissioned a robotic powder line for a Polish white-goods manufacturer — our first installation outside Asia.

2017

US footprint

Opened a forward-stock warehouse in Houston, Texas — a 72-hour spare-parts SLA across the continental United States.

2021

Yuehui app v1

Released the first version of the Yuehui mobile app on Google Play and the App Store — a free companion for every customer.

2024

São Paulo office

Latin American headquarters opened in São Paulo, with a regional engineering team covering Brazil, Argentina, Chile and Mexico.

2026

Today

62 countries served, 9,600+ installations completed, 24/7 hotline in five languages, and a team of 180+ across four continents.
